03 Bad photos

When the photo is bad

Blur, tilt, darken or compress the receipt photo in the same ways as the tests. Under each slider, you see the share of fields that the small model read correctly at the nearest tested damage level.

04 Honest scores

Is the confidence honest?

When the model says it is 80 percent sure, it should be right about 80 percent of the time. Each bar shows how often fields were right at one level of confidence, on the test receipts. Honest confidence puts every bar on the dashed line. Grey bars show the model's own confidence. Black bars show the same field after calibration.

05 Model choice

Which model reads it

A quick photo check picks one of two models before any model reads the receipt. One is the small model on a local computer, which costs nothing per photo. The other is Claude, which costs money for each photo.

    06 Your photo

    Check your own photo

    Drop a JPEG or PNG, or choose a file The photo stays in your browser. This page uploads nothing.
